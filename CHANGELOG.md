# Changelog

This file records only concrete, player-visible changes in each released version of Easy Red 2 Realism Overhaul. It is a living description of the shipped mod: superseded wording is replaced with the final behavior, and changes that are removed or reverted are deleted instead of retained as historical notes.

## 1.0.4 - 2026-07-24

Easy Red 2 compatibility: Steam public branch, build `24246380`.

- AI infantry now identify a point-blank threat noticeably faster than a merely "close" one, heavy suppression can no longer shrink a soldier's awareness of an immediate threat below a configurable minimum radius, and AI weapon spread now tightens the closer the target is inside close-quarters range, making room-to-room fights meaningfully deadlier.
- Crewed static AT guns now fire on enemy infantry when no vehicle target is available; vehicles still take priority.
- AI defenders now also man the gun of an empty armed troop transport parked inside their defend area, such as a halftrack or gun truck with a mounted machine gun, instead of only manning static emplacements. The soldier takes the gunner position rather than the nearest free seat, tanks, assault guns, and aircraft are never crewed this way, and the gunner gives the vehicle up as soon as a player orders a squad onto it or it drives away. It can be switched off with the new "Staff abandoned vehicle guns" defense setting.
- Rockets and other shaped-charge warheads now detonate against the first thing they strike instead of being carried through fences, sandbags, and thin walls by the prop-penetration system and re-appearing as a fresh projectile on the far side. This restores correct bazooka, Panzerschreck, Panzerfaust, and PIAT behavior, including the duplicate exhaust trail that appeared mid-flight. Solid armor-piercing shot and ordinary bullets still punch through props as before.
- Rocket exhaust trails are no longer stretched by the longer-smoke-effects setting, which left them smearing through the air after the warhead had already hit. Explosion and ground smoke keep their extended persistence.
- Removed the squad contact-reporting system (squad-local last-known-position reports and their voice/radio sharing between allied squads, with the related delay, confidence, and radio-error settings). AI now reacts only to what it actually sees or is shot by, which is simpler and cheaper; cover selection, defensive positioning, and APC dismount decisions still work from direct observation and incoming fire.
- Removed the experimental AI commander (attack/defense operation planning, staging gates, squad/vehicle/aircraft command roles): Easy Red 2's maps are balanced around vanilla's continuous frontal pressure, and the commander's staged gating made attacks stall instead of pressing the assault. Squads and vehicles now attack and defend under native game routing, with the existing infantry tactical layer (perception, suppression, danger reactions, cover) unchanged. AI staffing of defensive static weapons remains as a standalone behavior, no longer tied to the commander.
- Removed every aircraft system: AI attack safety and threat evasion, autonomous target-of-opportunity engagement, idle patrol re-centering, the whole flight model (momentum, stalls, spins, energy retention, damage effects, engine and speed tuning), and the flight instrument HUD, along with all of their settings. Planes now fly, fight, and handle exactly as they do in vanilla Easy Red 2 for both AI and players. Aircraft-bomb blast, crater, injury, and suppression tuning is unaffected and stays in the ordnance system.
- Close-range reactions no longer wait on the shared AI update queue: a soldier who sights an enemy inside surprise-contact distance confirms and engages after his normal short reaction delay even in large battles, so running riflemen no longer sprint obliviously past enemies directly in front of them for seconds at a time.
- Attackers caught crossing open ground under sustained trench fire no longer stall there forever: the maximum-halt escape that resumes a stuck advance now works while under direct fire in the open (it still holds a soldier in real cover until covering fire is established). A suppression pin now also releases after a bounded time even if incoming fire keeps suppression high, with a brief immunity window so the same fire cannot instantly re-pin the soldier before it can move.
- The mod no longer restricts the player's own actions anywhere: the stop-crawling-before-reloading-or-bandaging rule now applies only to AI soldiers, and a player who takes over a suppressed AI machine gunner can no longer inherit a stale duck latch that blocked standing back up at the gun.
- The AI fire-discipline and suppression systems now explicitly refuse to act on the locally controlled soldier even if the game misreports that soldier's control state after entering or leaving a vehicle, guarding against a reported permanent loss of the player's fire action after using a parked truck.
- Exiting a vehicle can leave a phantom seat reference on the player, which makes the game silently swallow every shot while the trigger animation still plays. The mod now detects that broken state on the next trigger pull and clears it through the game's own dismount path, restoring the player's ability to fire.
- Fixed a memory leak behind the persistent late-battle stutter: the mod's distant-sound and vehicle/cannon audio caches held on to every audio source they had ever seen, so with soldiers respawning all match the game's heap grew without bound and garbage-collection pauses grew with it. The caches now drop entries the game has already destroyed and reset at battle start. Audio behavior is unchanged.

## 1.0.3 - 2026-07-21

Easy Red 2 compatibility: Steam public branch, build `24246380`.

- AI tanks in armored contact now hold a genuine standoff position instead of stuttering forward and backward around the reverse threshold: the hold persists until the enemy is destroyed, breaks away, or stays out of sight, and a tank caught side-on at standoff range now rotates its hull to face the threat. Close-range and retreating tanks still keep their frontal armor toward the enemy and reverse straight back without pivoting. Turretless vehicles such as assault guns are exempt and keep hull-aiming naturally.
- Tank standoff and tactical reversing now trigger only against actual armored vehicles; tanks no longer keep their distance from, or back away from, unarmored trucks and jeeps.
- Before a tactical reverse, tanks now check for dismounted friendly soldiers and obstacles directly behind the hull and hold their ground instead of blindly backing over them, and a damaged tank's retreat now ends once it opens distance rather than looping indefinitely.
- Enemy infantry no longer freezes AI tanks in place: a tank with a live movement order keeps driving to its destination while its turret tracks the threat, and only a tank that is already parked halts to exploit its firing position.
- AI tanks that become stuck on obstacles while driving to a destination now attempt a short reverse to free themselves.
- Tank crews are no longer affected by the infantry armor-panic reaction to nearby enemy tanks, which could capture a crewman's movement decisions whenever enemy armor was close.
- Attacking infantry now search a much wider area for cover, so they reach flanking positions, doorways, and building corners instead of clinging to a narrow strip of ground directly ahead. The default cover search radius was raised and its upper limit extended for players who want an even broader search.
- Advancing soldiers may now peel off to cover that is slightly to the side or a few metres behind their line of advance, as long as it keeps them moving forward overall, rather than only accepting cover that is strictly closer to the objective. This opens up real flanking and building cover during an assault while still preventing them from retreating.
- Defending and staging squads are now placed onto the nearest cluster of protective cover—settling into a building or trench line—rather than standing on the open geometric point they were assigned. A squad already dug into good cover keeps its position. When a squad holds a dense line of cover, its members are allowed to spread along that whole building or trench instead of being compressed into a small circle of grass.
- Soldiers who genuinely cannot reach any protective cover now stay down and keep fighting, waiting progressively longer between attempts instead of endlessly milling in the open searching for cover that is not there.
- Defenders engaged from two directions at once now hold one sensible stance instead of rapidly flickering between crouch and prone. The stance is chosen against a stabilized threat direction that ignores brief bearing jitter and only turns to a genuinely new flanking attacker, and dropping from crouch to prone now requires the threat to persist rather than reacting to a single evaluation flip. Suppression and pinning keep their immediate drop to cover.
- Defenders holding a trench, window, or other authored fighting position now keep the stance the position was built for—crouched at the parapet, able to fire over it—instead of lying prone below it and going blind when the ground material cannot be classified. A confidently measured thin, penetrable barrier still sends them prone.
- Defenders now value being able to return fire when choosing cover. A position from which no stance can shoot back is now strongly avoided in favor of a well-protected position with an actual firing lane, so defenders no longer settle into protected but blind holes and stop shooting.
- AI soldiers must now keep an enemy in sight to identify them: brief glimpses separated by long gaps no longer add up to a confirmed target.
- Squads no longer replace fresher relayed contact intel with older sightings; newer information always wins, with first-hand reports preferred only on ties.

- Added a local, opt-in visual AI debug layer, toggled with **F8** by default. Its numbered, color-coded views can display live infantry perception and target memory, actual executor routes separately from movement ownership and planned cover, suppression and danger state, firing-lane safety, tactical arbitration winners, command leases, vehicle state, recent AI decisions, and one-second AI update timings. The sparse overview can be frozen, combined by layer, distance-limited, and focused on an individual soldier for expanded detail. Allegiance filtering applies immediately even to a frozen snapshot, uses the game's battle-side hostility rule across every entity-related layer, and exposes its player-faction reference plus visible/total counts in the header. The layer remains local and read-only without changing synchronized gameplay.
- Added rebindable binocular, freelook, and compass controls to the F10 settings menu. Binoculars still default to **Caps Lock**, freelook to either **Alt** key, and the five-second compass reveal to **K**.
- New installations and **RESET TO DEFAULTS** now use the current tuned gameplay, audio, visual, and player-suppression settings, including the high-quality distant-animation option. The melee-reach and tank-gun-volume sliders also provide headroom above those new defaults for further adjustment.
- Added **ENABLE ALL SYSTEMS** and **DISABLE ALL SYSTEMS** controls to the F10 settings menu. They stage every gameplay-system switch together, preserve personal display/diagnostic preferences and F10 access, and take effect when applied. **DISABLE ALL SYSTEMS** remembers which switches were off beforehand; **ENABLE ALL SYSTEMS** restores that saved mix instead of turning everything on, and a second click on **ENABLE ALL SYSTEMS** then enables every remaining switch.
- Defensive cover searches now inspect a deterministic spread across the entire search-area inventory, giving protected building and trench slots a fair chance to beat nearby exposed cover while keeping detailed ballistic evaluation bounded. Penetration-rated protection remains the first choice, but valid authored building and trench slots are retained as a conservative fallback when sampled material cannot be classified, instead of leaving soldiers at exposed arrival points.
- Attackers that complete a covered bound now retain their authored building or trench anchor even when the game's native cover flag briefly flickers off.
- Tactical pose ownership now latches the more protective posture immediately and prevents suppression or a valid protective assignment from churning soldiers between prone and crouched. Soldiers rise only after that owner releases them and the new posture remains valid for a stable interval.

## 1.0.2 - 2026-07-20

Easy Red 2 compatibility: Steam public branch, build `24246380`.


- Normal melee and bayonet attacks now consistently use the configured longer, wider hit area on the actual damage frame, instead of intermittently falling back to Easy Red 2's much shorter native range.
- Fixed the scrolling compass losing the map's true-north offset when the tactical map was closed or starting uncalibrated when it had never been opened. The compass now initializes from the current map's persistent north-direction setting, while the tactical map refreshes that reference whenever available.
- A host-authoritative ground director arbitrates squad, soldier, and emplacement orders using stable command leases, so AI tactical systems cannot repeatedly overwrite one another; player and mission-script orders always take priority.
- Defender static-weapon staffing now continuously fills every viable defend-area gun that available crews can reach, prioritizes AP-capable weapons when armour is reported, preserves leaders, key specialists, minimum squad strength, and a complete mobile reserve, and restores a protected gunner route if native AI clears it.
- Attacking infantry now use protected positions as deliberate bounds and stop to fight on contact, resuming the advance once covering fire is established or a bounded combat halt is reached.
- Fixed controller-owned soldiers being mistaken for AI when the first-person camera check was unavailable, which could block weapon fire and interfere with other player actions.
- Player-issued "Get In" orders now supersede automatic defender staffing on static weapons, and the first available squad member prioritizes the gunner seat so a partial AT-gun crew remains usable.
- AI squadmates under a player-issued hold order now spread into distinct protective positions within the ordered area and remain at those posts. If no viable slot is available, they hold locally and retry instead of wandering or accepting exposed ground as cover.
- Defenders now make one deliberate move from exposed arrival points—even when they already see an enemy—into genuinely protective trenches, buildings, or other fortified cover, prioritizing protection over an immediate firing lane and reserving enough physical space to prevent soldiers from stacking in the same position. Reached building and trench slots remain latched even when native cover-state reporting flickers or clears its destination.
- Defenders who reach useful cover now hold that position and let attackers come to them instead of circulating out into the open under fire. They relocate only when the defensive order changes or the position is destroyed, unsafe, or materially degraded.
- Unified cover and suppression posture control prevents AI from repeatedly switching between prone and crouched, while still allowing them to rise when a genuinely protective position has a usable firing line.
- Infantry cover scoring now measures material resistance, thickness, and protection across the whole body. Foliage, glass, canvas, and thin props no longer count like earthworks, sandbags, masonry, or substantial building cover.
- Simplified contact-response tuning by consolidating low-level cover timers, candidate limits, reservation spacing, and scoring into one stable policy; cover search radius remains adjustable.
- AI infantry now stop and briefly hold when a movement destination produces no real progress, preventing walking-in-place loops during cover movement and static-weapon transit. Repeated failures at the same destination produce progressively longer holds instead of constant retries.
- Reduced combat stutter by bounding expensive cover evaluation, removing allocation-heavy physics queries from hot paths, and throttling repeated contact-sharing and firing-lane work.
- Reduced combat stutter during dense firefights and barrages by spreading penetration-continuation bullets and cosmetic impact-hole spawns across frames, while capping the overhaul's supplemental explosion raycasts; Easy Red 2's native blast and fragmentation remain unchanged.
- Reduced recurring combat hitches by reusing tactical-resolution data instead of allocating it for every AI update, distributing gunfire-awareness scans across frames, avoiding repeated full cue-list cleanup on every shot, preventing overly frequent full defensive-emplacement inventories, and pacing smoke-persistence and explosion-audio updates during barrages.
- Reworked the local-player suppression blur so its intensity scales linearly with the suppression actually received, rapid hits stack, and the effect fully clears after incoming suppression stops.
- Fixed `VignetteMultiplier` so it reliably scales the native suppression vignette and applies setting changes immediately.
- Fixed repeated gun-audio null-reference errors by allowing distant-sound reflections and Easy Red 2's native weapon reverb to share one filter safely.
- Player suppression now supports a configurable expanded near-miss radius, allowing bullets outside Easy Red 2's native flyby radius to contribute suppression without double-counting native hits.
- Nearby allied wounds and deaths now cause a configurable morale shock for autonomous AI, with deaths producing the stronger and wider suppression effect.
- Allied multiplayer infantry can deliberately form one squad by selecting another allied player and choosing **Join [player]'s squad**. Joining moves only the current soldier, excludes vehicle crews, and must be chosen again after each respawn.
- AI with low-velocity anti-tank launchers keep their primary weapons equipped and withhold launcher fire beyond a configurable 90 m default (40–160 m); they switch to the launcher only after the target enters range, while high-velocity anti-tank rifles retain their normal range.
- AI tanks now clear stale steering when stopped, turn toward route nodes in a forward arc instead of spinning in place, and avoid pivoting their hull away from an armoured threat while the turret can continue tracking it.
- Added first-person binoculars toggled with **Caps Lock**, with a clean unmarked overlay, true configurable optical magnification that defaults to 10×, and automatic first-person weapon-model hiding while active.
- Added hold-to-freelook on either **Alt** key while alive and on foot. The horizontal arc defaults to 200° (100° to either side), is configurable, and smoothly recenters on release without turning the soldier or weapon.
- Added a map-north-aligned scrolling bottom-screen compass tape with tapered, fading edges. Pressing **K** shows it for five seconds; it defaults to NATO angular mils (0–6400), can use degree bearings instead, and has an option to remain permanently visible during gameplay.
- Reorganized AI controls into Infantry Tactics, Vehicle Tactics, Support Coordination, Attack Posture Bonuses, Defense, and Diagnostics pages. Existing values are migrated from the former sections.
