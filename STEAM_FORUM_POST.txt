Title: [Mod Release] Easy Red 2 Realism Overhaul

[h1]Easy Red 2 Realism Overhaul[/h1]

[b]Current version:[/b] 1.0
[b]Download:[/b] [url={{https://github.com/AtlasActual/Easy-Red-2-Realism-Overhaul}}]Easy Red 2 Realism Overhaul[/url]

I've been working on this overhaul to make Easy Red 2's battles feel less mechanical without changing what makes the game fun. Version 1.0 is ready for public release.

The main focus is battlefield behaviour. Infantry react more naturally to contact and suppression, squads work with imperfect information, and vehicles and aircraft make better tactical decisions. It is not a health or damage multiplier mod; the game's missions, armour system, and basic damage model remain intact.

[h2]Main features[/h2]

[list]
[*]Improved infantry awareness, suppressive fire, and movement under fire, with defenders strongly preferring protected trenches and buildings that retain useful firing lanes
[*]Suppression that slows reactions, limits awareness, pins exposed troops, and makes mounted gunners duck
[*]Squad contact reports passed by voice and radio instead of perfect enemy tracking
[*]Optional unified High Command coordination for attacks, flanks, reserves, armour, aircraft, artillery, smoke, and compulsory staffing of viable static weapons during defence
[*]Safer AI firing and grenade use, better anti-tank reactions, and more reliable melee hits
[*]Better tank positioning and reversing, plus safer aircraft targeting and configurable realistic flight physics
[*]Material-aware bullet penetration with energy loss, exit effects, tracers, and ricochets
[*]Reworked explosions, smoke and dust durations, audio rebalance, tracers quantity, battle chatter, and many more visual options
[*]True 10x Caps Lock binocular zoom without range markings and with the weapon model hidden, 200-degree hold-Alt freelook, and a map-aligned scrolling compass band shown for five seconds with K, faded edges, NATO mils by default, optional degrees, and an always-visible option
[*]A built-in settings menu available with [b]F10[/b] or from the main and pause menus
[/list]

Most features can be tuned or switched off, so you can keep the parts that suit the way you play.

[h2]Coming soon[/h2]

Tank ballistics, armour values, and internal vehicle subsystems are all being reworked for a future release. These changes are still work in progress and are not part of v1.0; for now, the mod leaves Easy Red 2's existing tank ballistics and armour model alone.

[h2]Installation[/h2]

ER2RealismOverhaul requires BepInEx 6 for Unity IL2CPP.

[olist]
[*]Download a build whose filename contains [b]Unity.IL2CPP-win-x64[/b] from the [url=https://builds.bepinex.dev/projects/bepinex_be]official BepInEx builds page[/url]. The [url=https://docs.bepinex.dev/master/articles/user_guide/installation/unity_il2cpp.html]official IL2CPP installation guide[/url] is also available.
[*]In Steam, right-click Easy Red 2 and select [b]Manage > Browse local files[/b].
[*]Extract BepInEx into that folder, beside [b]Easy Red 2.exe[/b].
[*]Start the game once, then close it. The first launch may take longer while BepInEx generates its files.
[*]Create [b]BepInEx\plugins\ER2RealismOverhaul[/b].
[*]Place [b]ER2RealismOverhaul.dll[/b] inside that folder.
[*]Start the game and press [b]F10[/b] to confirm the mod is loaded.
[/olist]

To update, close the game and replace the existing DLL. To uninstall, close the game and delete it. Saved settings are kept in [b]BepInEx\config\ca.antoi.er2.tacticalai.cfg[/b].

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]

Only the host needs the mod for its host-authoritative gameplay systems. Friends without BepInEx or ER2RealismOverhaul can join normally and experience the battlefield state synchronized by Easy Red 2, including the results of the host-controlled AI behaviour.

[list]
[*][b]Without the mod:[/b] Players do not receive the F10 menu, the host's settings, or local HUD, audio, and first-person options.
[*][b]With the same version:[/b] Players receive the host's settings for the session. Their menu stays read-only and their own configuration returns after disconnecting.
[/list]

If an unmodded player becomes host after the original host leaves, the host-authoritative overhaul systems stop running. A player with the same mod version should remain host.

[h2]Compatibility and feedback[/h2]

Version 1.0 was tested with the Easy Red 2 build available on July 19, 2026. Game updates can sometimes break BepInEx mods, so please report loading problems, unusual AI behaviour, multiplayer issues, or settings that need adjustment.
